About SARO

Our Mission & Vision

SARO – Save A Rupee Organisation was founded on a simple belief: small contributions create extraordinary change.

Even a single rupee — when pooled with thousands of others — can fund a child's meal, a community health camp, or a scholarship for a deserving student.

Our platform is transparent, student-driven, and community-focused. We believe in direct impact and the power of collective giving.
